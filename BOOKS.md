## C++ Books

### C++ Core Guidelines (English)

📘 [Cpp-Core-Guidelines-EN.pdf](books/C++/Cpp-Core-Guidelines-EN.pdf) · 532 pages · 8.2 MB

The **C++ Core Guidelines**, edited by Bjarne Stroustrup (creator of C++) and Herb Sutter, are the authoritative set of rules and best practices for writing safe, efficient and maintainable **modern C++**. Topics include resource management and RAII, interfaces, classes, error handling, concurrency, templates and generic programming. Ideal for intermediate to advanced developers and for teams defining a C++ coding standard.

A Chinese translation of the C++ Core Guidelines, useful for Chinese-speaking developers and teams adopting the guidelines.

### Modern C++ Tutorial: C++11 to C++26 On the Fly (English)

📘 [Modern-Cpp-Tutorial-C++11 to C++26 On the Fly-EN.pdf](books/C++/Modern-Cpp-Tutorial-C++11%20to%20C++26%20On%20the%20Fly-EN.pdf) · 112 pages · 3.8 MB

A compact, hands-on guide to **modern C++ features from C++11 through C++26**: `auto` and type deduction, lambdas, move semantics and rvalue references, smart pointers, `constexpr`, variadic templates, the concurrency library and more. Perfect for developers who know "classic" C++ and want to catch up quickly.

## Rust Books

### The Rust Programming Language (English)

📘 [The-Rust-Programming-Language-EN_v1.98.pdf](books/RUST/The-Rust-Programming-Language-EN_v1.98.pdf) · 573 pages · 17 MB

Known as **"The Book"**, this is the official introduction to Rust, written by Steve Klabnik, Carol Nichols and the Rust community (edition for Rust 1.98).
